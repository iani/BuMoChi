---

**Do not delete the rejected options, even for a short version.** They are the
most valuable part of the document. Under time pressure the temptation is to
record only what you built — but "why not the simpler thing?" is the first
question anyone asks, and the answer is only cheap to write down while it is
still fresh.

If you do not know a value, write a placeholder like `<TBD>`. Never invent a
name, a benchmark or a citation to fill a gap.
